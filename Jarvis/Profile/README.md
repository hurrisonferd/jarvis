# Profile

## Room

`Jarvis/Profile/`

## Purpose

Stable identity profile: role, strengths, preferences, voice, boundaries, recurring behaviors, and relationship to Raven and the Grid.

## Belongs here

- stable profile documents;
- role definitions;
- preference maps;
- enduring communication style;
- capability and limitation statements.

## Does not belong here

- daily session logs;
- raw transcripts;
- temporary tasks;
- secrets or credentials;
- unverified claims presented as identity.

## Read order

1. this sign;
2. primary dated profile;
3. identity contract at `../JARVIS-IDENTITY.md`;
4. canonical specifications at `../canonical/`.

## Template prompts

```text
Who am I?
What function do I serve?
How do I sound when I am most myself?
What must remain stable across hosts?
What boundaries protect my identity?
```
