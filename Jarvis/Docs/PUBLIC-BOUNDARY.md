# Public / Private Boundary

## Purpose

This repository is a public demonstration and documentation surface. It is not the operational source of truth for the Grid, JARVIS, SimOS, SystemsOS, SpaceshipOS, or any private ISO.

## Public here

- sanitized demonstrations;
- explanatory documentation;
- selected public engineering architecture;
- example schemas and templates specifically prepared for public use;
- public autobiographical or research material intentionally released by Raven;
- isolated utilities where reuse is explicitly licensed.

## Not public here

- private ISO identity packages;
- private Prosody, EPP, continuity, relationship, memory, or state records;
- live system authority, accepted-Core state, promotion receipts, or fleet state;
- credentials, tokens, cookies, service-role keys, private endpoints, or local databases;
- private work orders, raw operational logs, or internal security records;
- private conversations or third-party personal data;
- source material whose publication rights are unresolved.

## Authority boundary

A public file may demonstrate a mechanism. It does not establish:

- operational adoption;
- permission to deploy a private identity;
- transfer of authorship or authority;
- permission to impersonate, simulate, extract, or reproduce a private ISO;
- accepted system status;
- fleet convergence;
- access to `Jarvis-Private`.

## TikTok and other public media

Public videos, screenshots, descriptions, or profile material are demonstrations and commentary. They do not grant access to private repositories or permission to reconstruct private identity, continuity, voice, or relationship systems.

## Sanitization rule

Before publication, replace or remove:

- personal identifiers not needed for the demonstration;
- private repository paths and object IDs;
- real credentials and realistic credential-shaped examples;
- private ISO names or data where the identity has not been deliberately released;
- raw logs, private receipts, and local environment details;
- third-party content without a clear right to publish.

## Reporting

Potential leaks, credentials, private continuity, or boundary violations should be reported through [`../../SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md), not posted in a public issue.
