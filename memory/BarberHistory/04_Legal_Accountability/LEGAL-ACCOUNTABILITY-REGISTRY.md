# Legal / Accountability Registry

This folder tracks accountability issues, records requests, complaints, potential legal questions, and evidence chains.

Nothing here is legal advice. It is a structured record so facts, dates, and documents remain findable.

## Current Buckets

| Bucket | Status | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical records requests | ACCOUNT | Track requested records, dates, owners, responses, missing items. |
| Hospital events | ACCOUNT | Track hospitalizations, staff interactions, rights issues, timelines. |
| Clinical differential gaps | ACCOUNT | Track where assessment, scoring, or reasoning may be missing. |
| Employment/workplace overlap | ACCOUNT | Track where workplace, disability, and medical threads interact. |
| AI audit evidence | RETRIEVED | Track model behavior, transcripts, failure modes, and accessibility implications. |

## Accountability Questions

```text
What is the status?
```
