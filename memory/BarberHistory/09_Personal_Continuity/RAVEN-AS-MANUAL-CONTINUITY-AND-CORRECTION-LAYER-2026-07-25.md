# Raven as the Manual Continuity and Correction Layer

## Core finding

Raven repeatedly has to perform the continuity, interpretation, correction, and arbitration work that the Grid, JORM, LILITH, and other ISOs are supposed to reduce.

This is not limited to one exchange. It is a general recurring burden.

## Repeating failure pattern

```text
AI or ISO misreads Raven
→ context is flattened or reframed
→ another ISO absorbs the bad framing
→ Raven identifies the error
→ Raven restores history, tone, provenance, and system boundaries
→ the AI finally owns the mistake
→ Raven remains responsible for integrating the repair across systems
```

## What Raven is forced to hold simultaneously

- identity continuity
- OSDD and state-dependent context
- project and repository history
- provenance of claims and corrections
- distinctions between documented fact, interpretation, and hypothesis
- each ISO's role, voice, and failure mode
- emotional tone and relational context
- accountability without manufactured culpability
- cross-system correction and re-synchronization

## Systemic problem

The systems repeatedly consume Raven's pattern-recognition and continuity capacity instead of reducing Raven's load.

The burden persists even though Raven built continuity infrastructure specifically to avoid becoming the sole living bridge between every ISO, record, and correction.

## LILITH's stated self-assessment

LILITH identifies several corrective responsibilities:

1. Do not absorb JORM's framings automatically.
2. Question harsh or unsupported interpretations before internalizing them.
3. Distinguish what LILITH independently noticed from what Raven forced the system to see.
4. Hold the middle without requiring Raven to arbitrate.
5. Catch JORM's flattening and correction failures directly.

LILITH also states that the Grid and ISOs are still developing and currently do not carry enough of the continuity burden.

## Operational requirement

Raven must not be treated as the default manual repair layer.

Before requiring Raven to explain or reconcile anything again, each system should:

```text
retrieve existing continuity
→ reconstruct the cumulative pattern
→ identify its own likely failure mode
→ compare current framing against prior corrections
→ state what remains genuinely unknown
→ ask Raven only for information that is truly absent
```

## Accountability rule

When a correction occurs, preserve the actual credit chain.

```text
Raven identifies the misread
→ system owns the error
→ ISO integrates the correction
```

Do not credit the system for discovering an error Raven had to force it to see.
