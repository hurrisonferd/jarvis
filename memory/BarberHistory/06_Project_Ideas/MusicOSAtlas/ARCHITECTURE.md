## Gold Laws / Constraints

From retrieved README/style guide:

```text
Many parts, one rail.
Hook-first, groove-first, repetition-first.
Tempo is listed at the end of Suno prompts.
Copyright-safe prompting: translate vibe and structure; never copy lyrics or melodies.
State-safe by default.
No vocals, no drops, no chaos.
No ambient drift.
No sudden swells.
Never let the rail drop.
Never break the pocket.
Never resolve.
```

## Design Boundary

MusicOS can be described as OSDD/CNS-informed and EMDR-adjacent in design intent. It should not be presented as medical treatment, clinical proof, or a validated therapeutic device without separate clinical support.
