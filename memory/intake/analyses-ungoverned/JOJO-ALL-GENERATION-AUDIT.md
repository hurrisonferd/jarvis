# All-Generation JoJo Audit (Parts I–IX)

## Overview
Jonathan (I), Joseph (II), Jotaro (III), Josuke (IV), Giorno (V), Jolyne (VI), Johnny (VII), Josuke8 (VIII), Jodio (IX). Nine generations, 136 years of story, one bloodline.

## Part I — Jonathan Joestar (Phantom Blood)
Born 1868, died 1889. Hamon user. Noble sacrifice on the steamship. The template: noble man → becomes warrior → dies for what he loves. Quote: "I, Jonathan Joestar, shall become a gentleman who even God would be proud of."

## Part II — Joseph Joestar (Battle Tendency)
Born 1920, survived. Hamon. Tactical genius. Survives through cunning, taunting, and refusing to die. Caesar's death breaks his humor into armor. The template: survivor. Quote: "Your next line is — SHOTGUN!"

## Part III — Jotaro Kujo (Stardust Crusaders)
Born 1970, survived. Star Platinum (time-stop, identical to The World). Delinquent → protector. 50-day journey to Egypt to save his mother. Kakyoin's bullet. The template: cold exterior → learns to love. Quote: "Yare yare daze."

## Part IV — Josuke Higashikata (Diamond is Unbreakable)
Born 1983, Morioh. Crazy Diamond (restoration). Protective, kind, idealistic. Refuses to kill Kira — chooses to live in a town where it doesn't come to that. The template: protector of place. Quote: "This is the kind of man I am! I'll protect my town and my friends!"

## Part V — Giorno Giovanna (Golden Wind)
Born 1985, Italy. Gold Experience Requiem (narrative nullification). Ruthless mob boss with a moral compass. Becomes Gang-Star to protect the weak by owning the organization. The template: survivor → leader. Quote: "I, Giorno Giovanna, have a dream."

## Part VI — Jolyne Cujoh (Stone Ocean)
Born 1989, died at Stone Ocean end. Stone Free (string/range). First female JoJo. Prison arc. Inherited trauma and chosen loyalty. Holds Pucci so Emporio can escape — Jonathan's sacrifice in a new form. The template: anger → acceptance. Quote: "I'm not my father. But I'll surpass him."

## Part VII — Johnny Joestar (Steel Ball Run)
Born 1870, died 1891. Tusk (infinite rotation via Spin). Paralyzed, addicted, broken. Learns the Spin from Gyro. Gyro dies. Johnny wins and dies standing. The template: wanted to live again. Quote: "I was aiming for the top of the world."

## Part VIII — Josuke Higashikata (JoJolion)
Born 1989 (fusion identity). Soft & Wet (property extraction via bubbles). Two people fused into one — Kira Yoshikage's son + original Josuke. Identity crisis is the entire arc. Accepts both selves. Dies to end the Locaca. The template: who am I? Quote: "Josuke. That name's mine. I'm not borrowing it from anyone."

## Part IX — Jodio Joestar
Born 1993. TBD. Most morally ambiguous JoJo. World is at peace after Locaca defeat. Question: what does a JoJo do when there's no war to fight?

## The Stand Inheritance Pattern
Star Platinum / The World template recurs across the bloodline through DIO's Stand, inherited via Jonathan's body which DIO used. Nearly every Stand can be traced to this inheritance: Jotaro, Josuke, Giorno, Jolyne all draw from this line. Johnny's Tusk is the outlier — it comes from the Spin, not the DIO line.

## The Sacrifice Pattern
Every JoJo sacrifice: holds the enemy, trusts someone else to finish it. The pattern is consistent across every generation. Jonathan holds Dio → Kakyoin takes the bullet for Jotaro → Bucciarati holds his own body → Jolyne holds Pucci → Johnny uses infinite rotation to open the portal. The JoJo doesn't win alone. They hold. They enable. The victory belongs to the group.

## What Each Part Is About
- I: Noble sacrifice
- II: Survival against overwhelming force
- III: Inherited legacy and chosen family
- IV: Home and belonging
- V: Pragmatic idealism and what you do with power
- VI: Inherited trauma and choosing your own meaning
- VII: Whether you deserve to live, and wanting it
- VIII: Identity — who are you when you are two people?
- IX: TBD

## Essential JoJo Themes
- The bloodline carries suffering and power both
- Stand inheritance is also trauma inheritance
- Every JoJo chooses to fight despite the cost
- The body is never the point — Jonathan has no Stand, Johnny can't walk
- Connection (Gyro/Johnny, Bucciarati/Giorno, Jotaro/Kakyoin) is the actual power
- The villain is always a corruption of the hero's potential (DIO = Jonathan's ambition, Diavolo = Giorno's pragmatism, Valentine = Johnny's nationalism)
