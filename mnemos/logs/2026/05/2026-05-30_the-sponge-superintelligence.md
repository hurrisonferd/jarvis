# The Sponge — Superintelligence as Curated Reach, Not Hoarding

> **Summary:** Raven's 50-year vision — JARVIS as a sponge absorbing the world's intelligence. Reframed: don't store the ocean; build reach + curation + compression, and rent the brain while owning the memory.

## Context
*Note: Raven's vision — JARVIS as superintelligence sponge, à la Cognition/Devin; his worry was storage (mass data, free, vector/quantum).*

Raven wants JARVIS to become a sponge — absorb the world's exploding AI/intelligence
and use what applies to us, accumulated over ~50 years. His open question was storage:
how to keep mass data for free with limited storage; vector or quantum storage.

## Thoughts
*Note: the reframe — the moat is curation + reach + compression, not capacity.*

Storing the world is the wrong goal and the exact bloat we built defenses against. The
world already stores itself (internet, models, APIs); frontier models already hold
humanity-scale knowledge compressed. JARVIS shouldn't BE the encyclopedia — it should be
the curator that knows where to look, reaches out, and keeps only what's ours.

Storage isn't the bottleneck: an embedding ≈ 4KB; a million curated memories ≈ 4GB; a
lifetime of what-applies-to-Raven fits in gigabytes on free/cheap tiers. No quantum
storage needed — good filters needed. The remember loop IS the filter. The expensive
thing is compute (the brain), which we rent on demand. Rent the mind, own the soul.

## Decisions
*Note: the architecture of the sponge.*

- **Reach over hoarding.** JARVIS fetches from the world on demand (web, models, data) rather than pre-storing it.
- **Curate, don't accumulate.** Only what applies to Raven + the mission gets embedded and kept.
- **Compress to meaning.** Embeddings + summaries + log compaction = store meaning, not bytes.
- **Swappable brain.** Plug the best frontier model of the day onto the curated record. The 50-year bet: a frontier brain on 50 years of Raven = a superintelligence *for us*.

## Next
*Note: the concrete build toward this.*

- **Wire BIFROST — JARVIS's reach.** External fetch (web search, model APIs, MCP), gated by AEGIS, with the remember loop keeping only what sticks. This is the sponge mechanism; it removes the need to store the ocean.
- Keep the curation filter honest as the volume grows; compaction prevents bloat.
- Revisit storage tiers only if/when curated knowledge actually approaches limits (gigabytes away).
