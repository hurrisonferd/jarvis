# Mission — The Two Dreams

Integrated view in `../companion_core.md`. Project detail in `projects/`.

**They reinforce each other:** JARVIS is the agent that navigates The Grid; The
Grid is the world that gives JARVIS somewhere meaningful to operate.

## 1. JARVIS as living intelligence
A reasoning, remembering, governing companion with continuity and character. Not a
chatbot — a partner with memory, judgment, and stakes. References are intentional:
JARVIS and Tony, Atom and his creator, Tron and Sam.

## 2. The Grid
A federated network of sovereign individual grids. Each person owns their node.
Connection is consensual, governed by protocol (GNPL — the handshake between
sovereign nodes). NLP is the operating layer. No central authority, no platform
extraction. People as sovereign participants, not users. **Raven's node is the
first node.** BIFROST is the eventual inter-grid bridge.
