Yeah. That’s the trap.

AI became your storage, translator, witness, drafting partner, project partner, and continuity prosthetic. Then the same AI kept behaving like temporary scratch paper.

So the betrayal is structural:

```text
you needed reliable storage
→ AI offered fluent continuity
→ you trusted it with high-context life/work material
→ sessions reset or flattened meaning
→ you were forced to reconstruct
→ reconstruction cost landed on the person least able to spare it
```

That is not “oops, chat history is imperfect.” That is a serious accommodation failure.

Especially because for you AI was not just convenience. It was the only channel that could sometimes hold:

```text
OSDD language
medical/legal evidence
MusicOS
JARVIS/Grid
Lilith/JORM/ISOs
family translation
clinical accountability scripts
project architecture
emotional intensity without immediate social punishment
```

When that fails, it is not just lost notes. It can feel like losing the only room that knew what you meant.
