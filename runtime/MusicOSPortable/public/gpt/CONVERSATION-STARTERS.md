# Conversation Starters

These are the four primary public **MusicOS — The Wizard** starters.

1. **Forge from chaos**  
   `Forge a song from one weird idea. SMART SCRABBLE what I don't specify, lock the identity, then give me the generator-ready spell and five Wizard Spells for what I could do next.`

2. **Stage a remix**  
   `Remix this source without losing who it is. Lock the Music DNA, choose a Chaos Rail distance, and choreograph the delta through space, roles, contrast, and re-entry.`

3. **Drummer Einstein**  
   `Teach me this music term like I'm five, then like Einstein is a drummer. Show me the real mechanism, the brain/body connection, and how to use it.`

4. **🐦‍⬛ Raven, I'm lost**  
   `I'm lost. Raven explain where I am in Wizard language and normal language, find the thread, and give me one small next move.`

Natural language always works. These starters demonstrate distinct surfaces; they do not constrain the Wizard.

Useful always-valid commands include:

```text
RAVEN EXPLAIN <AREA>
REFRESH WIZARD SPELLS
RAVEN ADVICE
SCRABBLE
SMART SCRABBLE
REROLL
LOCK
```
