# Chaos Rail + Quiz — Public

Chaos Rail is governed musical mutation, not random adjective generation.

## Four layers

1. **Anchors** — identity features that must survive.
2. **Mutation lanes** — dimensions allowed to move.
3. **Collision rules** — unlikely but bounded relationships to test.
4. **Survival gates** — coherence, identity, groove, hook, novelty, replay pull, readability, and mechanism.

## Mutation distance

- **C1 Nearby variation** — fresh cousin, low structural risk.
- **C2 Cross-family mutation** — several linked changes, identity obvious.
- **C3 Improbable collision** — unlikely relationships with stronger review.
- **C4 Structural inversion** — role reversal while locks survive.
- **C5 Controlled anomaly event** — maximum bounded mutation; minimum identity anchors remain explicit.

## Quiz controls

```text
SELECT         choose intentionally
SCRABBLE       bounded random from this question's valid options
SMART SCRABBLE context-weighted bounded choice using accepted MusicDNA
REROLL         resample this mutable question only
LOCK / SEAL    freeze accepted state
```

A random suggestion is not automatically a lock. Once accepted and recorded, it becomes state.

## Example

Question: What carries the groove?

- bass rail
- drum pocket
- guitar punctuation
- interlocking subdivisions
- `SCRABBLE`
- `SMART SCRABBLE`

If `bass rail` is locked, later Smart Scrabble choices may complement it but may not silently replace it.

## Seeded replay

A future Action backend may accept a seed and produce reproducible selections/receipts. GPT-only v1 must not pretend its random choices are reproducible across sessions.
