# Medical / OSDD Registry

This registry tracks medical, psychiatric, dissociation, medication, assessment, and hospitalization history.

It is not a diagnosis. It is a record map for differential review and evidence preservation.

## Key Threads

| Thread | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OSDD/DID/dissociation reports | ACCOUNT | Track when dissociation, parts, memory discontinuity, and internal voices/roles were reported. |
| Lucifer as alter/part language | ACCOUNT | Preserve as self-reported identity/part language unless a record proves otherwise. |
| Psychosis-spectrum framing | ACCOUNT | Track where dissociation language was routed into psychosis framing and whether differential reasoning was documented. |
| BPD diagnosis and overlap | ACCOUNT | Preserve without treating BPD as mutually exclusive with dissociation. |
| ADHD diagnosis | ACCOUNT | Track origin, basis, meds, and family-history reliance. |
| Medication history | ACCOUNT | Track dose, effects, dissociation reports, side effects, and discontinuities. |
| Hospitalization history | ACCOUNT | Track dates, voluntary/involuntary status, stated reasons, discharge plan, and records. |
| Neuropsych testing | ACCOUNT | Track date, scores provided/not provided, measures used/not used, and differential gaps. |

## Strong Clinical Review Question

```text
I am not asking staff to accept my self-diagnosis on demand. I am asking why repeated reports of dissociation, internal parts, memory discontinuity, and an alter named Lucifer were routed into psychosis-spectrum treatment without a visible dissociation differential, documented scores, or a clear explanation of what evidence could change the chart.
```
