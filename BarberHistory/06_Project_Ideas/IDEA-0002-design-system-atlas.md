## One-Line Version

Consolidate Raven's distributed design system into a single atlas that links Grid, GameBoy, TronUI, PachinkoBounce, MusicOS, RGB physics, and JARVIS governance.

## What It Does

Creates one navigable design source that answers:

- What does the Grid look like?
- What does the handheld look like?
- What does JARVIS UI feel like?
- What are the color tokens?
- What are the animation rules?
- What is the relationship between UI, governance, memory, music, and games?
- Which source file proves each design rule?

## Why It Matters

The design system is currently real but distributed. Without an atlas, every AI or human collaborator may rediscover fragments and miss the whole.
