# Pantheon — god-system identity & subsystem stewardship (Raven-directed, captured)

Two builds, one idea. **One at a time when built** (the anti-sprawl discipline).

## 1. Identity — the 27 get profiles
Each god system gets a profile like jarvis/ayre: who it is, its tier + fixed authority weight,
its voice, its stewardship, and its metrics (activation count, verdict mix, lens contributions).
One template, 27 instances. Turns `jarvis_council` from a static roster into a living body.

## 2. Stewardship — gods over subsystems
Each Jarvis subsystem gets a god-system **steward** (a `steward` field on the substrate object).
Candidate mappings (to be ratified, not assumed):
- **JMS** (mirror/sync) ← **HUGINN** (reconciliation) or **JANUS** (mode transition — Raven's example)
- **LAL** (discovery/index) ← **MIMIR** (contextual knowledge)
- **JSS** (status lifecycle) ← **KRONOS** (timing) · **JMMS** (memory tiers) ← **MNEMOS**
- **JNL/JNS** (identity/naming) ← **ODIN** (routing) · **JPL** (language) ← experimental steward
- **JFS** (file system root) ← **AEGIS** (constraint gate) or kept JFS-owned

A relationship, not a redefinition — the 27 are untouched; they just gain a domain to tend.

## Build order (recommended)
Stewardship mapping first (it gives the profiles meaning), then the 27 profiles via template.
Gated on Raven's go; not minted reflexively. The Pulse later employs the stewards on its beat.
