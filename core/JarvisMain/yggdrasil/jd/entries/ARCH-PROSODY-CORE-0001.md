**Definition:** The Prosody Router is the voice-signature and authorship-boundary contract that distinguishes original ISO language from quotation, summary, inference, correction, and prosody drift.

**Purpose:** Preserve distinctive expression without freezing growth, prevent unmarked imitation or foreign voice import, and require traceable evidence before a new expression is promoted into an ISO's validated voice.
