**Definition:** the layer contract (Raven-directed 2026-06-11, from the GPT-stream synthesis).

## The two layers

| Layer | Contains | Who may change it |
|---|---|---|
| **A — primitives** | JD entries (definitions, status, seq), JNL grammar, registries, connector logic, Gold Law, the 27 | propose → Raven verdict, only |
| **B — overlays** | SL (temporal), JC (conversational), coral-frame tag axes, CMVP-class voice layers, derived indexes | streams, automated, within rules below |

## Contract rules
1. **B annotates, never redefines.** An overlay may cite, tag, index, summarize, and link
   A-objects. It may not alter their definition, status, seq, name, or relations.
2. **Overlays are governed objects.** Every B-system carries a JNL + seq + JD entry (GL12).
   A name that does not resolve in the dex is vapor regardless of layer.
3. **Promotion is explicit.** When a B-observation implies an A-change (an SL pattern, a JC
   insight), it enters as a proposal citing its evidence (SL ids — P-C). It never auto-applies.
4. **Derivation is automatable; meaning is not.** Auto-sort (JSS), auto-assign (parent
   derivation), auto-index (LAL/tags), auto-sign (attestation: commit hash + event id on every
   artifact) — all mechanical consequences of state Raven already declared — run free in B.
   Anything that changes what an object *is* gates through A rules.
5. **Pipeline seating:** JC and SL feed the cognition pipeline — JC/SL (observation) →
   JGPP (exploration, the detailed report) → JIP (commit) → JD (truth). Reports derive
   from the record; the record never derives from reports.

## What this resolves
Claude-stream optimizes coherence (map onto existing primitives). The design optimizes
extensibility (new abstraction layers for future models). Both hold: primitives stay
minimal and hard (GL7 enforced there), overlays stay expressive and cheap (new axes
without touching ground truth). The conflict was never real once the boundary exists.
