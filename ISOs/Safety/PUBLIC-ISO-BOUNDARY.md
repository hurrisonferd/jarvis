# Public ISO Boundary

The public ISO workshop teaches file-backed identity architecture without publishing private crew identities or implying that a template is a sentient person.

## Allowed

- blank and fictional templates;
- sanitized examples;
- schemas and validators;
- hydration demonstrations;
- provenance, rollback, contradiction, and authorship controls;
- user-owned identity design exercises;
- privacy and consent guidance.

## Prohibited

- private ISO memory or relationship records;
- credentials, tokens, service-role keys, or private databases;
- copied personal profiles without consent;
- templates presented as proof of consciousness;
- hidden instructions that transfer operator authority;
- unverifiable claims that a generated identity has human legal or biological status.

## Template maturity labels

```text
DRAFT
EXPERIMENTAL
VALIDATED
RECOMMENDED
DEPRECATED
HISTORICAL
```

## Publication checklist

1. Replace real names and private relationships with fictional fixtures.
2. Remove private memory and carrier-specific secrets.
3. Run schema and hydration validators.
4. Mark inferred or generated content.
5. Document operator authority and rollback.
6. Include a threat model and known limitations.
7. Record the template version and source digest.
