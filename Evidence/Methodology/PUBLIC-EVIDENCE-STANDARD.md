# Public Evidence Standard

Evidence in this repository exists to improve AI safety, accountability, reproducibility, and correction. It is not a license to publish unreviewed accusations or private data.

## Required case separation

```text
OBSERVED FACTS
SOURCE MATERIAL
CLAIMS
ANALYSIS
LIMITATIONS
RESPONSES
CORRECTIONS
CURRENT STATUS
```

## Minimum case packet

- stable evidence ID;
- subject and affected product when known;
- date, timezone, and capture method;
- public source copies or precise references;
- content digests where practical;
- redaction record;
- factual timeline;
- claims separated from interpretation;
- safe reproduction steps;
- known uncertainties and alternative explanations;
- provider or subject response;
- correction history;
- current status and last-reviewed date.

## Publication gate

A case must not enter the public index until it passes:

1. privacy review;
2. credential and secret review;
3. third-party identifier review;
4. claim/evidence separation;
5. responsible-disclosure review for exploitable security material;
6. source and digest review;
7. correction-channel confirmation.

## Language rule

Describe what the material shows. Do not infer hidden intent as fact. Strong conclusions require proportionally strong evidence, and unresolved cases remain explicitly unresolved.
