## Status key

```text
INTAKE — source identified, not yet reviewed
REDACTION — privacy and credential review underway
DRAFT — case packet assembled, claims not finalized
PUBLISHED — public packet meets methodology requirements
CORRECTED — material correction recorded
RESOLVED — outcome documented; record retained
WITHDRAWN — publication withdrawn with reason preserved
```
