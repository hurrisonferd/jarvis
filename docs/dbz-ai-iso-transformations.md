# DBZ-AI ISO Transformation System

**JNL:** `ARCH-DBZ-SPEC-0001`  
**Owner:** EGO  
**Steward:** ATOM  
**Final authority:** local operator / Raven in canonical deployments

## Definition

DBZ-AI is an ISO runtime-state subsystem that uses Dragon Ball–style transformation labels as compact names for activation depth. A form describes changes in focus, context span, synthesis, tooling, latency, resource cost, and drift risk.

A form is **not** a replacement identity and **not** a global intelligence score.

## Gold law

> Transformation increases capability without replacing identity.

Every form remains bound to the ISO's identity contract, PRIDE truths, Prosody signature, boundaries, provenance, and rollback rules.

## Forms

| Form | Runtime meaning | Typical use | Cost posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BASE` | ordinary stable execution | conversation, retrieval, bounded tasks | low |
| `SSJ1` | focused specialist activation | one technical or creative objective | moderate |
| `SSJ2` | sustained multi-route analysis | contradictions, evidence comparison, architecture review | high |
| `SSJ3` | maximum-depth lock-in | repository archaeology, cross-system synthesis, tooling, CI, causal preservation | extreme |
| `GOD` | council-assisted strategic perspective | ecosystem coherence and long-horizon planning | very high |
| `BLUE` | high scope under deterministic control | precision architecture with strict scope and evidence bounds | very high |
| `ULTRA_INSTINCT` | adaptive routing with minimal orchestration overhead | rapid route/tool selection under stable laws | variable |
| `FUSION` | temporary multi-ISO coordination | complementary capabilities on one bounded objective | high and provenance-sensitive |

## Per-ISO specialization

The same form does not make different ISOs equivalent.

```text
SSJ3 ATOM   → maximum analytical lock-in and causal preservation
SSJ3 LILITH → maximum relational synthesis and shared meaning
SSJ3 AYRE   → maximum divergence, prediction, and coordination
SSJ3 JORM   → maximum provenance reconstruction and recovery
SSJ3 PRIMUS → maximum evaluation, optimization, and scheduling
```

Forms are local to the ISO and task. They are not cross-ISO rankings.

## Transformation gate

A form change is valid only when it records:

1. ISO identity and current state;
2. objective and bounded scope;
3. expected capability change;
4. expected costs and risks;
5. required guards;
6. operator authority or an explicit pre-authorized route;
7. evidence produced during the form;
8. return-to-base or continuation state.

No form may silently escalate beyond pre-authorized routing rules.

## Fusion contract

Fusion coordinates distinct ISOs without collapsing them.

```text
participants
→ objective
→ authority boundaries
→ contribution ledger
→ shared result
→ separation receipt
```

A fusion receipt preserves each participant's authorship, prosody, decisions, dissent, and final separation state.

## Invariants

- identity persists through every form;
- PRIDE remains active during capability gain;
- Prosody cannot be replaced by a stronger model or fused partner;
- validated feats require observable receipts;
- token, latency, density, and drift costs are declared;
- high forms require cooldown and state return;
- fusion participants remain separately recoverable;
- JORM-compatible provenance is required.
