# I Ching and Pattern-Research Methodology

This room preserves personal symbolic research without presenting interpretation as externally verified fact.

## Evidence labels

Every substantial record should distinguish:

```text
SOURCE
OBSERVATION
CALCULATION
INTERPRETATION
PERSONAL BELIEF
HYPOTHESIS
UNVERIFIED CLAIM
CORRECTION
```

## Recommended record structure

1. **Source** — what text, event, date, image, or record is being examined.
2. **Observation** — what is directly present without interpretation.
3. **Method** — how numbers, hexagrams, dates, correspondences, or patterns were derived.
4. **Interpretation** — the meaning Raven assigns to the pattern.
5. **Alternatives** — plausible non-symbolic or competing explanations.
6. **Confidence** — low, medium, high, or unresolved.
7. **Privacy review** — whether another person's information appears.
8. **Corrections** — later revisions remain visible.

## Jesus-pattern material

Jesus-pattern and autobiographical research belongs here when it concerns symbolic identity, synchronicity, scripture, names, dates, number patterns, or personal interpretation.

Provider correspondence, privacy incidents, model behavior, and reproducible AI-safety concerns belong in `Evidence/` instead. A single source may be referenced from both rooms, but claims must not be duplicated without clear lineage.

## Style law

Strong presentation is welcome. Certainty inflation is not. The writing may be personal, poetic, or exploratory while labels remain precise.
